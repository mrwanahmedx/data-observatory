How to interpret the metrics

ROC AUC measures ranking; average precision summarizes precision at recall increments, not trapezoidal PR area. Brier loss measures probability error and combines discrimination and calibration. Lower Brier alone does not establish calibration. The baseline predicts training prevalence for everyone. No confidence intervals or real-world validity are claimed.
